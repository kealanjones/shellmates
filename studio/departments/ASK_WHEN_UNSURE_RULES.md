# Ask-When-Unsure Rules — Decision Matrix

## A) Must Ask Before Deciding
- Changing core vision or north star.
- Changing couples-first positioning.
- Changing asynchronous-first model.
- Choosing backend provider.
- Implementing account pairing.
- Storing personal relationship data.
- Adding analytics or monetisation.
- Adding push notifications.
- Adding relationship scoring.
- Changing art direction.
- Changing story canon.
- Adding deep repair/conflict prompts.
- Generating large content batches.
- Changing long-term progression model.

**Example:** “Should we add a ‘relationship health’ meter?” → Must ask (forbidden pattern risk).

## B) May Decide Independently
- Small file organisation within approved structure.
- Placeholder names for temporary assets/data.
- Local helper functions within approved implementation scope.
- Minor copy improvements that do not change tone or meaning.
- Small reversible implementation choices.

**Example:** Renaming a temporary schema field for consistency before any content import.

## C) Must Document in Decision Log
- Any lasting change in workflow or approval process.
- Any schema-breaking or milestone-impacting decision.
- Any resolved ambiguity that future agents might encounter.

**Example:** Confirming portrait-first remains provisional through Milestone 1.

## D) Must Escalate to Studio Director
- Conflicting guidance across bibles.
- Safety vs schedule conflict.
- Any request pressuring bypass of quality gates.
- Scope expansion that threatens milestone integrity.

**Example:** Proposal to skip relationship safety review to hit deadline.

## Escalation Template
1. Decision needed.
2. Why it matters.
3. Options with trade-offs.
4. Recommended option.
5. Impact on milestone and gates.
