# PR Review Guidelines

## Every PR Must Include
- Problem statement.
- Scope and explicit non-goals.
- Quality gate impact summary.
- Risks and mitigations.
- Validation evidence (commands/checks).
- Decision log update note (if applicable).

## Review Priorities
1. Product vision and north-star alignment.
2. Couples-first and relationship safety compliance.
3. Mobile-first usability and readability.
4. Asynchronous progression integrity.
5. Technical quality and deferred-system compliance.
6. Documentation consistency and milestone fit.

## Blocking Issues
- Violates relationship safety.
- Violates product vision.
- Breaks mobile-first principles.
- Crosses privacy boundaries.
- Introduces forbidden early systems (backend/account/analytics/monetisation/push).
- Missing acceptance criteria or unclear ownership.

## Warning Issues
- Tone drift risk.
- Partial schema compliance.
- Insufficient QA evidence.
- Ambiguous milestone impact.

## Rework Triggers
- Any blocker unresolved.
- Multiple warnings without mitigation.
- Contradiction with bibles/pipelines.

## When to Update Bibles
- New lasting product rules.
- New tone constraints.
- New technical constraints.
- New relationship safety guardrails.

## When to Update Decision Log
- Directional decisions.
- Deferred/approved system boundary changes.
- Milestone scope pivots.

## Documentation-Only PR Rule
Documentation-only PRs still require full quality review and must pass relevant gates.
