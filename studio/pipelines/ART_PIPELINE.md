# Art Pipeline — From Placeholder to Production

## Stages
1. Art brief creation.
2. Placeholder creation (temporary).
3. Production art requirements definition.
4. Sprite scale decision (explicit approval).
5. Palette decision (explicit approval).
6. Animation requirement pass.
7. Mobile readability check.
8. Naming/folder conventions validation.
9. Integration review.
10. Final approval.

## Rules
- Placeholder assets are temporary by default.
- Art style changes require explicit approval.
- Lobster assets require heightened expressive standards (emotion legibility first).

## Art Brief Minimums
- Purpose in gameplay.
- Emotional target.
- Required states/poses.
- Readability constraints.
- Integration dependencies.
