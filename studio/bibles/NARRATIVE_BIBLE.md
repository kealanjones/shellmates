# Narrative Bible — Shellmates

## Narrative Scope
Story guidance for episodic chapter writing and world consistency without over-expanding lore.

## Setting
- **The Cove:** shared home hub where memory and customisation accumulate.
- **Sea Glass Isles:** magical island chain for chapter adventures and discoveries.

## Core World Force: The Drift
- The Drift is a magical force of distance, fog, distraction, and disconnection.
- It is not moral judgement and not a “relationship punishment”.

## Tone
- Cute, silly, warm, romantic, occasionally tense.
- Tension should invite teamwork, not fear.

## Humour Style
- Playful, whimsical, affectionate.
- Favour surprising magical details and character quirks.

## NPC Style
- Memorable personalities with gentle eccentricity.
- Offer guidance, wonder, or comic relief without sounding clinical.

## Episode Theme Examples
- The Lantern That Forgot (noticing each other)
- The Market of Mixed Messages (playful misunderstanding)
- The Cave That Echoed Wrong (listening)
- Storm Night at the Cove (support during stress)
- The Message Bottle Reef (surprise and appreciation)
- The Far Tide Bridge (shared future)

## Tension Style
- Fog, storms, distance, lost letters, separated lobsters, fragile lanterns.
- Avoid grim horror, emotional punishment, or villainised partners.

## Canonical Rules
- Emotional themes must be framed as magical adventure.
- Avoid blunt labels like “Communication Island”.
- Both partners should retain agency in chapter outcomes.

## What Not To Do
- No direct therapy framing or clinical lesson language.
- No shame-based narrative beats.
- No deterministic “better/worse partner” framing.
