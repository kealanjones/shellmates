# Game Bible — Shellmates

## One-Paragraph Pitch
Shellmates is a couples-first asynchronous mobile pixel art adventure where two partners build a shared seaside Cove, explore the magical Sea Glass Isles, and progress through story chapters together over months. Each partner can make meaningful solo progress at any time, while major story progression unlocks when both contribute. The experience is cosy, playful, and emotionally meaningful, designed to foster connection through play rather than therapy.

## Core Fantasy
- We are tiny adventurers building a magical life together by the sea.
- Our lobsters are loyal companions: mascots, messengers, and puzzle helpers.
- Even when apart, we can leave traces that help each other move forward.

## Target Player Experience
- Frequent short sessions with low friction.
- Warm delight from shared discoveries and small affectionate gestures.
- Ongoing sense of “our world grows when we both show up”.

## Product Pillars
1. Asynchronous Togetherness
2. Shared Home Building (The Cove)
3. Episodic Adventure (Sea Glass Isles)
4. Playful Emotional Expression
5. Sustainable Long-Term Progression

## Core Spaces
### The Cove (Homepage Hub)
- Default landing location.
- Hosts decoration, memory displays, rituals, shell notes, and chapter status.
- Must always provide at least one meaningful action regardless of partner availability.

### Sea Glass Isles (Story Mode)
- Chapter-based exploration and puzzle progression.
- Contains My Path, Their Path, optional support actions, and shared Tidegate resolution.

## Lobster Companions
- Emotional messengers: carry small notes, tokens, and playful signals.
- Mascots: reinforce warmth and identity.
- Puzzle helpers: interact with chapter mechanics in lightweight ways.

## Standard Asynchronous Chapter Structure
1. Shared story hook
2. My Path (solo beats for current player)
3. Their Path (parallel solo beats for partner)
4. Optional support actions (helpful but non-blocking)
5. Our Tidegate (requires both partners’ contribution)
6. Shared resolution scene
7. Memory reward (Keepsake)
8. Next unlock signal

## Progression Model
### Solo Progress
- Exploration, resource gathering, notes, customisation, and prep actions.
- Always rewarding, never framed as “lesser”.

### Shared Progress
- Main story unlocks, major chapter outcomes, Tide Marks, and key Keepsakes.
- Requires participation from both partners but with flexible timing.

## Long-Term Progression
- Episodic chapters over many months.
- Seasonal overlays and Cove evolution.
- Growing memory collection and décor identity.

## Daily/Weekly Rhythm
- Daily: 2–10 minute check-ins, one to three meaningful actions.
- Weekly: chapter steps, shared unlock moment, Cove updates.

## Core Resources
- **Glow:** earned via play, notes, prompts, and story actions.
- **Sea Glass:** earned via exploration/challenges; spent on décor and cosmetics.
- **Tide Marks:** earned from shared chapter completion; used for major story unlocks.
- **Keepsakes:** unique memory objects from important couple moments.

## Emotional Tone
- Cute, silly, warm, romantic, occasionally tense.
- Tension should resolve into collaboration and care.

## Humour Rules
- Gentle whimsy, absurd seaside magic, affectionate banter.
- Avoid sarcasm that implies blame or contempt.

## Tension Rules
- Use magical obstacles: fog, drifting currents, lost lantern light, separated paths.
- Never present relationship conflict as a score or judgement.

## Must Avoid
- Love score or relationship health metrics.
- Guilt-based reminders.
- Therapy positioning or clinical framing.
- Surveillance-feeling mechanics.

## Example Normal Session Flow
1. Open Cove and collect a partner trace left earlier.
2. Complete one My Path step on current chapter.
3. Send optional support action via lobster.
4. Place a new décor item.
5. Exit with clear next action and warm tone cue.
