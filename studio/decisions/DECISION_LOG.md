# Decision Log

## Format
- **Date:**
- **Decision:**
- **Reason:**
- **Alternatives considered:**
- **Risks:**
- **Revisit when:**

## Initial Decisions

### D-0001
- Date: 2026-05-05
- Decision: Couples-first, not general friendship-first.
- Reason: Product identity and emotional framing depend on partner-specific dynamics.
- Alternatives considered: Generic social co-play framing.
- Risks: May narrow audience breadth.
- Revisit when: Post-vertical-slice audience validation.

### D-0002
- Date: 2026-05-05
- Decision: Asynchronous-first, not live co-op-first.
- Reason: Supports real-world schedule mismatch and long-distance use.
- Alternatives considered: Live co-op dependent progression.
- Risks: Async clarity complexity.
- Revisit when: After M1 usability findings.

### D-0003
- Date: 2026-05-05
- Decision: Mobile-first product strategy.
- Reason: Session pattern and relationship rituals suit frequent phone usage.
- Alternatives considered: PC-first or parity-first.
- Risks: UI readability and performance constraints.
- Revisit when: After device test data in M1.

### D-0004

- Risks: Future tooling integration constraints.
- Revisit when: End of M1 technical review.
