# Shellmates Agent Operating Manual

## 1) Project Summary
Shellmates is a couples-first asynchronous mobile pixel art adventure for Android and iOS. Two partners create tiny characters and lobster companions, build a shared seaside Cove, and explore the Sea Glass Isles. Solo progress should remain rewarding, while main story progress requires contributions from both partners.

## 2) Design North Star
**Connection through play, not therapy.**

## 3) Product Positioning (Non-Negotiable)
- Shellmates is **couples-first**, not general friendship-first.
- Shellmates is **asynchronous-first**, not live co-op-first.
- The game must never shame either partner for inactivity.
- Do not use relationship health scores, love percentages, compatibility ratings, or punitive streaks.
- Deeper prompts must be opt-in, swappable, or clearly signposted.
- Use magical world language instead of guilt language.

## 4) Operating Priorities
1. Mobile-first design and validation.
2. Couples-first safety and tone.
3. Asynchronous progression clarity.
4. Data-driven content structure before content scale.
5. Reversible decisions until milestones prove assumptions.

## 5) Source-of-Truth Folders
- `studio/bibles/` — product, design, narrative, art, technical, relationship foundations.
- `studio/departments/` — ownership, role boundaries, escalation rules.
- `studio/schemas/` — content schema contracts.
- `studio/pipelines/` — authoring and review workflows.
- `studio/reviews/` — quality gates and PR standards.
- `studio/milestones/` — scope and exit criteria by phase.
- `studio/decisions/` — documented directional decisions and revisit triggers.

## 6) Product Rules
- Prioritise short, repeatable mobile sessions.
- Keep solo actions meaningful even when partner is unavailable.
- Require both partners for major chapter progression via shared gates.
- Keep long-term progression seasonal/episodic without pressure mechanics.

## 7) Relationship Safety Rules
- No blame framing towards either partner.
- No forced deep emotional prompts during light play.
- No therapeutic claims, diagnosis framing, or treatment framing.
- Brave/deeper prompts must be clearly optional and reversible.

## 8) Technical Rules (Current Phase)
- Do not implement backend, account pairing, analytics, monetisation, push notifications, or storage of personal relationship data without explicit approval.
- Assume Godot + GDScript for planning; do not start production implementation unless milestone authorises it.
- Keep systems local-first and prototype-safe until technical direction changes via decision log.

## 9) Content Rules
- Do not generate large content batches until schemas, pipelines, and quality gates are approved.
- Use small reviewed batches for prompts, chapter beats, and items.
- Keep content varied, specific, and playful; avoid bland filler and therapy-coded phrasing.

## 10) Tone Rules
- Default tone: cute, silly, warm, romantic, occasionally tense.
- Tension should be magical and metaphorical (fog, storms, drifting lanterns), not grim or punitive.
- Keep copy concise and readable on mobile.

## 11) Ask-When-Unsure Rules
Agents must ask before changing any of the following:
- Core vision or north star.
- Relationship safety policy.
- Backend/privacy/account pairing direction.
- Monetisation or analytics plans.
- Platform strategy.
- Art direction.
- Story canon.
- Long-term progression model.

## 12) Output Standard for Every Task
Every task response must include:
1. Summary of files changed and why.
2. Risks/assumptions.
3. Validation commands run.
4. Next recommended tasks.
5. Citations to changed files where relevant.

## 13) PR Review Priorities
1. Vision and couples-first alignment.
2. Relationship safety and tone safety.
3. Mobile-first usability.
4. Asynchronous progression clarity.
5. Technical prudence and deferred-systems compliance.
6. Documentation consistency and decision-log updates.

## 14) Forbidden Early Work
Until explicitly approved:
- No backend selection or integration.
- No account-pairing implementation.
- No analytics, monetisation, or push notification implementation.
- No personal relationship data architecture.
- No large content generation waves.
- No final art production lock-in.
